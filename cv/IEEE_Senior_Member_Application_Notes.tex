\cvsection{No fixed official template}
ieee.org does \textbf{not} publish a mandatory ``Senior Member CV template'' file. The actual official requirement lives inside the online application system at \href{https://nominate.ieee.org}{nominate.ieee.org}, which is a set of structured form fields rather than a single document. Some Regions (e.g., Region 3, Region 5) publish their own \textit{recommended} (not mandatory) resume templates to help candidates prepare, but these are volunteer-created aids, not ieee.org requirements.

\cvsection{The application has four tabs}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Application Info / Nominee Information} --- basic info; pre-fills once your IEEE member number is entered.
  \item \textbf{Professional Experience} (Education + Employment) --- a \textbf{structured field}, not a free-form upload. For each entry you must provide:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Organization name and address
    \item Start date \textbf{and} end date, both to the \textbf{month and year} (not just the year)
    \item Job title / degree
    \item A description of duties and responsibilities
  \end{itemize}
  Reviewers specifically check that start/end dates make sense and do not illogically overlap (e.g., two full-time roles at once without explanation).
  \item \textbf{Significant Performance} --- a \textbf{separate free-text box} (limit: 4997 characters), not the same as the CV attachment. Must describe discrete projects using: \textit{what you did (task) $\to$ your specific role $\to$ why it was significant (quantified impact) $\to$ time period}. Do \textbf{not} put awards/honors/thank-yous here. Dates need not be consecutive but must sum to $\geq$ 60 months. Do not pad past $\sim$70 months --- reviewers explicitly discourage over-filling this field.
  \item \textbf{Attachments} --- this is where you \textbf{upload a PDF resume/CV}. A resume is mandatory; the format is not strictly enforced, but Region committees strongly encourage using month/year precision and detailed duty descriptions consistent with the Professional Experience tab, since a well-structured resume makes independent verification much easier for reviewers and reference-writers.
  \item \textbf{References} --- up to 4 references; you need their IEEE membership numbers. If self-nominating, 3 references are required; if nominated by a Senior Member/Fellow, only 2 additional referrals are needed (the nomination itself counts as one).
\end{itemize}

\cvsection{Relationship between the CV attachment and the online fields}
The uploaded CV/resume PDF (Attachments tab) is a \textbf{supplementary document}, not a substitute for filling in the Professional Experience and Significant Performance fields directly in the system. \textbf{Both must be completed}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item The \textbf{Significant Performance text box} in the online form is what the Admission \& Advancement (A\&A) Review Panel actually reads and scores against the eligibility criteria --- it must be filled in directly on the website.
  \item The attached CV is useful context for reviewers and, especially, for your references/nominator, who may quote or draw on it when writing their referral. Uploading the ``Tian\_Lan\_CV\_IEEE\_SeniorMember.pdf'' document (which already contains a Significant Performance section written in the recommended task/role/why-significant/duration format) makes it very easy to copy-paste/adapt that section into the online text box when the time comes --- but the copy-paste step itself still needs to happen in the system.
\end{itemize}

\cvsection{Eligibility quick-check (IEEE Bylaw I-104.3)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Ten years of professional practice in an IEEE-designated field (Computer Science / AI qualifies). Degree credit: Bachelor's = 3 yrs, Bachelor's+Master's = 4 yrs, Doctorate = 5 yrs. Credited years and actual work/school years for the \textbf{same period} cannot both be counted --- choose whichever combination gives the larger, most defensible total.
  \item Five years (60+ months, not necessarily consecutive) of significant performance within that practice.
  \item Three references, each currently holding IEEE Senior Member, Fellow, or Honorary Member grade.
  \item No extra fee for the elevation itself; you must already be (or become) a paying IEEE Member first.
\end{itemize}

\cvsection{Process overview}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Join/renew IEEE Membership (if not already a member).
  \item Log in to \href{https://nominate.ieee.org}{nominate.ieee.org}; fill in Professional Experience, Education, Significant Performance, and Present Occupation tabs.
  \item Upload the CV/resume PDF under Attachments.
  \item Add 3 references (their IEEE numbers); wait for their status to change from ``Notified'' to ``Complete'' --- do \textbf{not} click Submit before that.
  \item Submit at least 10 days before the next Region review-panel date.
  \item Track status via the Senior Member Application Status Report; approval typically follows within $\sim$2 weeks of the panel review.
\end{enumerate}
